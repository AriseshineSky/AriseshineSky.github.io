## III. Teaching Material: Vocabulary & Dialogues

### A. Core Vocabulary (Modules 1 & 2)

| English Term | Focus | Example Sentence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clerk / Agent** | Personnel | Please speak to the **clerk** at Window 3. |
| **Apply for / Renew** | Action Verbs | I need to **apply for** a license, not **renew** it. |
| **Fee / Fine** | Money | The application **fee** is $50, but a speeding **fine** is $150. |
| **Expired / Valid** | Status | This license is **expired**. Is your passport still **valid**? |
| **Vehicle Registration** | Document | You must show the **vehicle registration** for your car. |
| **Title / Pink Slip** | Ownership | The **pink slip** proves you own the car. |
| **VIN** | Identifier | The **VIN** is located on the dashboard. |

### B. Road Test Commands (Module 2)

| Command Category | Command / Feedback |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Initial** | Please **start the engine**. / Adjust your **mirrors**. |
| **Maneuver** | **Signal right** and change lanes. / Perform a **three-point turn**. |
| **Parking** | **Parallel park** behind that blue car. / You failed to check your **blind spot**. |
| **Safety** | Come to a **complete stop**. / You were **driving too slowly**. |

### C. Real-Scenario Dialogue: Applying for a New License

| Character | Dialogue | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Applicant (A)** | Good morning. I'd like to **apply for a new driver's license**. | *Stating the purpose.* |
| **Clerk (C)** | Do you have your **appointment confirmation** and all the **required documents**? | *Checking requirements.* |
| **A** | Yes. Here is my passport, a bank statement for **proof of residence**, and my completed **application form**. | *Listing documents.* |
| **C** | Thank you. Your name will be called for the **vision and knowledge tests**. You will pay the **fee** afterward. | *Giving instructions.* |

### D. Real-Scenario Dialogue: The Vision Test (Module 3)

| Character | Dialogue | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clerk (Giving Commands)** | Please step up to the machine. Cover your **right eye**. | *Initial instruction.* |
| **Applicant (Responding)** | Okay. Can you point to the **smallest line** I should read? | *Asking for help/clarification.* |
| **Clerk (Testing)** | Read the letters on the **third line from the top**. | *Specific testing command.* |
| **Applicant (Clarity)** | **Could you please repeat** that number? | *Using polite modals to ask for repetition.* |

### E. Violations Vocabulary (Module 4)

* **Traffic Ticket:** A notice of a violation and fine.
* **Speeding:** Driving faster than the posted limit.
* **DUI (Driving Under Influence):** Driving while intoxicated (very serious).
* **Suspended License:** Temporarily taken away due to violations.
* **Tow Truck:** A vehicle used to move cars that are disabled or illegally parked.
